Deleting your account

In the game

  1. Open the game while your device is online.
  2. Press the gear at the top left.
  3. Open Accounts.
  4. Press Delete account, then Delete.

Your account is deleted straight away, and the app starts again as a new player. This cannot be undone.

If you cannot open the game

Write to xiusprimary@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my account” and your player name with its tag. We may ask a question or two to be sure the account is yours, and we delete it within 30 days.

What is deleted

Everything kept with the account: your name, tag and character, your coins, gems and experience, what you own and what stands on your plot, your coin history, your friends and friend requests, and every message to or from you. Nightly backups of the server are overwritten, and a deleted account is gone from them within 30 days. Nothing else is kept. The privacy policy has the whole list.

Common questions

The game says “A new version is out”

Update the app from the store you got it from. Until then it plays offline: the town is there, and the people in it are the computer’s.

It will not go online

Check that your device has a connection, and that the app is the newest version. The game keeps trying by itself while you play, so there is nothing to press. Some school and office networks stop games; try another network or mobile data.

How do I change my name?

Press the gear at the top left, open Accounts, and press the pencil beside your name. A name can be in English, Korean or Vietnamese.

I have a new phone, or I removed the app. Where is my account?

An account belongs to the app on one device, and there is no sign-in yet, so it cannot be moved to another device, and a fresh install starts as a new player. If you would like the old account deleted, write to us with its name and tag.

Someone is being rude, or cheating

Write to us with their player name and tag, what happened and about when. We look into every report.

My coins or my things are wrong

Write to us with your player name and tag, and what you expected to have. The server keeps a history of every change to your coins, so we can look.
