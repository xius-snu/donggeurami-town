Privacy Policy

Donggeurami Town (동그라미타운) · Last updated 24 November 2026

The short version

  • You play without giving us an email address, a phone number or your real name.
  • The game has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not sell or share your data.
  • Our server keeps only what an online game needs: your player name, your character, your progress, your friends, and messages waiting to be read.
  • You can delete your account, and everything kept with it, from inside the game at any time.

1. Who we are

Donggeurami Town (“the game”) is made and run by Hajun Park, an independent developer (“we”, “us”). This policy covers the game on every device it runs on, and the online service it connects to.

Questions about this policy or about your data: xiusprimary@gmail.com.

2. What is kept on our server

The first time the game goes online it makes an account for your device by itself. There is nothing to sign up for. The account holds:

An account key

A random number the app makes up the first time it opens. It is not your device’s hardware ID or advertising ID, and it says nothing about you. The server keeps only a one-way hash of it.

Why: to know which account is yours without a password.

Your profile

The player name you type, the four-digit tag after it (like #1234), and the character you pick.

Why: so other players can see you and find you.

Your progress

Your coins, gems and experience, the things you own, what you have put on your plot of land and how you painted it, and a history of your coins (how many, the reason, such as a game’s fee or prize, and when).

Why: to save your game and keep balances right.

Friends and messages

Your friends list, friend requests that have not been answered, and messages sent between friends. A message is removed from the server once the person it was for has read it and closed it.

Why: so the friends features work.

Dates

When the account was made and when it was last online.

Why: to run the service, and to show your friends how long you have been away.

3. What passes through while you play

  • What you do in the game. Where your character is and what you do in the town and in a minigame are sent to the server and on to the players in the same place, so that you can play together. They are not saved afterwards.
  • Chat. A line of chat goes to the players in the same town or game. It passes through a filter for common swear words. We do not save or log chat.
  • The server’s log. The server writes a working log of events, such as a player name joining or leaving and how a game went. We use it to keep the server running and to find faults. It is overwritten as it fills up.
  • Your IP address. Like every online service, the server sees the internet address you connect from. It is used to carry the connection and to turn away floods of requests. It is not saved in your account.

The app also keeps a few small files on your own device: your account key, your name and character, and your game for playing offline. On an iPhone or iPad it keeps a log of its last launch in its own folder, which stays on your device unless you choose to send it to us. All of these are removed when you delete the app.

4. What we do not collect

  • Your real name, email address, phone number or age. The game never asks.
  • Your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • Advertising IDs or hardware IDs.
  • Payment details. Nothing in the game is sold for real money.

The only permission the app asks for is internet access. It contains no advertising or analytics code from other companies.

5. How we use it

Only to run the game: to save and load your account, to show your name, character and plot to other players, to deliver friend requests and messages, to keep the game fair, and to find and fix faults. We do not use it for advertising, we do not build profiles of players, and we never sell it.

6. What other players can see

  • Your player name and tag, and your character.
  • Your plot of land and what you have built and painted on it. Your plot, with your name on its sign, can stand in the town while you are away, with a computer character under your name (“your name’s bot”) walking about on it.
  • Whatever you write in chat, which goes to everyone in the same town or game. A message is seen only by the friend you send it to.
  • Your friends can see whether you are online, or how long you have been offline.

Please do not put personal information, yours or anyone else’s, in your name, in chat or in messages.

7. Who we share it with

Nobody. We do not sell, rent or trade player data.

The game’s server is a machine we rent from Vultr, a hosting company, in its Seoul data centre, so your account is stored in South Korea. We would hand data to an authority only where the law requires it. Apple and Google run the stores you download the game from, under their own privacy policies.

8. How long we keep it

  • Your account: until you delete it.
  • Messages: until the person they were for has read and closed them.
  • Backups: the database is backed up every night so that nobody’s game is lost if the server breaks. A deleted account is gone from the backups within 30 days.

9. Deleting your account, and your other choices

Delete your account in the game: press the gear at the top left, open Accounts, press Delete account, then Delete. Your device has to be online. This removes your profile, your progress, your coin history, your friends and requests, and every message to or from you, straight away, and the app starts again as a new player.

If you cannot open the game (for example, you have already removed it), write to us with your player name and tag and we will delete the account for you. The steps are on the support page.

Change your name at any time: the gear, Accounts, then the pencil beside your name.

Play offline. With no internet connection the game still plays, alone, and nothing is sent.

You may also ask us what is kept about your account, ask for a copy of it, or ask us to correct it, by writing to xiusprimary@gmail.com. Please include your player name and tag. We answer within 30 days.

10. Keeping it safe

The connection between the app and the server is encrypted. The server keeps only a hash of your account key, so a copy of the database would not let anyone into an account. The database cannot be reached from the internet, and only the developer can get into the server. No online service can promise perfect security; if you think something is wrong, please tell us.

11. Children

The game does not ask for your age, and it works without a real name or any contact details. It has no ads and nothing to buy with real money. Players can talk to each other in chat and in messages to friends, so please take care not to share personal information there.

If you are under 14, or under the age at which you can agree to this yourself where you live, ask a parent or guardian before you play online. Parents and guardians: you can delete your child’s account in the game, or write to us and we will do it.

12. This website

These pages set no cookies and load nothing from other sites. They are hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log visits, including IP addresses, as its own privacy statement describes.

13. Changes to this policy

When the game changes what it keeps, this page changes with it, and the date at the top with it. If a change is a large one, we will say so in the game’s update notes.
